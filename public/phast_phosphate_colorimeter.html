Higher phosphate concentration deepens the blue solution, reducing transmitted red intensity according to the Beer-Lambert Law (A = ε·b·c).

SCIENTIFIC MANUAL & SPECTROPHOTOMETRY PRINCIPLES
PHAST Instrument Architecture

1. Chemical Reaction Mechanism

Orthophosphate reacts with acidified ammonium heptamolybdate and antimony potassium tartrate to form 12-molybdophosphoric acid: 

H₃PO₄ + 12(NH₄)₂MoO₄ + 21H⁺ ⟶ (NH₄)₃[PMo₁₂O₄₀] + 21NH₄⁺ + 12H₂O

Ascorbic acid reduces this heteropoly acid to produce molybdenum blue (mixed-valence heteropoly complex), which strongly absorbs red light in the 650–880 nm spectrum.

2. Beer-Lambert Colorimetry

Under monochromatic or bandpass red illumination, transmitted light decreases logarithmically with concentration: 

A_R = -log₁₀(I / I₀) = ε · b · c

By dividing measured intensity I by blank reference I₀, ambient drift is eliminated and concentration is computed from the calibration slope: c = (A_R - b) / m.
